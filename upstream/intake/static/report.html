Upstream first glass

One photo of the first water of each supply helps find leaks before people get sick.

  1. When the water comes on, fill a clear glass with the first water.
  2. Stand it on the card's circle; dip a chlorine strip and lay it in the slot.
  3. Photograph the whole card, all four corner squares in view.
